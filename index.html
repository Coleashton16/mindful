About the Creator

I'm Cole Ashton, a Registered Nurse and founder of Allied Technologies, Allied Tech Services, and AlliedRNPrep. I started Allied Technologies during my nursing school college career, and it has since flourished into multiple platforms built for real people.

My work sits at the intersection of healthcare and software. Over the years, I've designed and launched a portfolio of practical applications built for the people who keep healthcare moving—including a comprehensive nursing study platform powered by a progressive CAT NCLEX engine, a bedside nurse aid for managing shift workflows, and a schedule-matching app built to keep busy friends connected.

Why I Built Mindful

Mindful is my most ambitious project yet. Powered by a custom multi-engine AI system, Mindful was built from the ground up to deliver responsive, structured therapeutic support at zero cost.

Mental health care shouldn't be a luxury reserved for those who can afford out-of-pocket sessions. My goal with Mindful is simple: harness modern AI to close the accessibility gap and put meaningful support within everyone's reach.
