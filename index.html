Why structured sessions work better than 24/7 AI chat

Open-ended chatbots can feel repetitive and lose track of your story. Mindful is designed around healthy boundaries and steady, real-world progress.

A Healthy Rhythm

Sessions happen during scheduled appointment times so you have space in daily life to practice what you've learned—with an urgent check-in option if a tough moment hits unexpectedly.

Sees the Whole You

Your physical health affects how you feel. A quick yearly check-in lets you note sleep habits, medications, and optional health records so your sessions stay grounded in your real life.

Clear Progress Over Time

Every session wraps up with an easy-to-read summary, a practical weekly exercise, and a clean printable PDF you can keep for yourself or share with your doctor anytime.

How It Works

Simple, supportive steps from day one

01

Quick Health Profile

Start with a friendly conversation about your sleep, everyday habits, medications, or optional health records so Mindful knows your full story.

02

Book Your Sessions

Pick a 45-minute appointment time that fits your week. Your private session room opens 10 minutes before you begin.

03

Check-In & Recap

Finish each session with a 30-second mood check-in while Mindful saves a clear, easy-to-read summary of what you covered.

04

Personal Care Plan

Watch your progress over time, review your personal goals, and try one small, realistic practice between appointments.

What's Inside

Six specialized AI systems working together for you

Instead of relying on one generic chatbot, Mindful uses six focused systems behind the scenes to keep your support thoughtful, consistent, and organized.

01 · CONVERSATION

Your Guided Therapist

Leads calm, paced 45-minute sessions tailored to your personal goals, story, and current week.

02 · RECAP

Session Summaries

Turns every session into a clear, organized note so you never have to start from scratch next time.

03 · PROGRESS

Care Plan & Goals

Tracks how you're feeling over time, updates your goals, and suggests one simple exercise for the week ahead.

04 · SUPPORT

Health Profile & Urgent Help

Walks you through your yearly health check-in and helps unlock an urgent session if you're having an especially hard day.

05 · SCHEDULING

Smart Pacing

Recommends a healthy session rhythm—offering extra support when things feel heavy and spacing out visits as you feel stronger.

06 · QUALITY

Ongoing Care Review

Reviews completed sessions behind the scenes to keep conversations natural, respectful, and consistently helpful.

About the Creator

I'm Cole Ashton, a Registered Nurse and founder of Allied Tech Services and AlliedRNPrep. I originally created Allied Technologies during my nursing school college career, and it has since flourished into a suite of multiple platforms built at the intersection of healthcare and software.

Over the years, I've designed and launched a portfolio of practical applications built for the people who keep healthcare moving—including a comprehensive nursing study platform powered by a progressive CAT NCLEX engine, a bedside nurse aid for managing shift workflows, and a schedule-matching app built to keep busy friends connected.

Why I Built Mindful

Mindful is my most ambitious project yet. Powered by a custom multi-engine AI system, Mindful was built from the ground up to deliver responsive, structured therapeutic support at zero cost.

Mental health care shouldn't be a luxury reserved for those who can afford out-of-pocket sessions. My goal with Mindful is simple: harness modern AI to close the accessibility gap and put meaningful, clinically structured support within everyone's reach.
